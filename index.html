03 / Syllabus & Guidance

Trinity Exam Tracks & Curriculum

Click any discipline to view graded repertoire, technical elements, and digital exam supervision.

Classical Guitar Track

Grades Initial – Grade 8 • Sight Reading & Counterpoint

Standard notation sight-reading, classical right-hand mechanics (tirando and apoyando strokes), nail shape tone production, polyphony, arpeggios, and canonical pieces from Sor, Carcassi, and Brouwer.

Full guidance provided for Trinity digital video exam single-take filming and acoustic balance at no extra charge.

Acoustic Guitar Track

Grades Initial – Grade 8 • Fingerstyle & Contemporary Repertoire

Plectrum velocity, Travis picking, modern percussive fingerstyle, altered tunings (DADGAD, Drop D), chord voicings, and contemporary dynamic nuance tailored to Trinity acoustic assessment criteria.

Includes guidance on backing track integration, recording volume staging, and video exam submission protocols.

Rock & Pop Track

Grades Initial – Grade 8 • Electric Lead, Riffs & Band Staging

Electric riffs, pentatonic/modal solos, string bends, vibrato mastery, palm muting, tone dialing, and playing tightly locked with real backing tracks.

Covers amp level balancing, audio interface inputs, and digital exam filming compliance.
